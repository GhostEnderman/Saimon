01 · From the heart

A letter for you

For all the things I feel, even when I cannot find the perfect words.

My dearest Sairar Ma,

Since the first time I saw you, my love for you has never decreased. If anything, it keeps finding new ways to grow.

We found each other at a time when we have very little time together. Our relationship is so different, and somehow, that makes our story feel like something that belongs only to us.

I always feel that you are with me, and then I have no fear. Even on the days when things feel difficult, the thought of you gives me a kind of peace I cannot explain.

I love your eyes, your hands, your hair. I love everything about you, because at the end of the day, you are mine. 🥺

Maybe I cannot give you every moment we wish we had, but I want to make the moments we do get count. I want to keep learning you, choosing you, and finding my way back to you—again and again.

If forever is made from little promises kept every day, then I hope we spend our time keeping them together.

Always yours, Saimon ♡
